More Ways to Shape Your Library

The first round of Your Library, Your Voice listening sessions has wrapped, and the conversations were everything we hoped for. Now we're opening up two new ways to help shape the renovated library: more listening sessions — including late-evening virtual ones — and brand-new focus groups built around the things you care about most.

Over the past few weeks, residents came together in the library to talk about what they love about the Main Library and the Bellevue Avenue Branch and what they hope these buildings can become. The turnout was wonderful and the conversations were honest, generous, and full of ideas. Every one of them is going straight into the design and community-engagement process with DIG Architecture — and the more voices we gather now, the better the design that follows.

What we heard

More listening sessions

Two things became clear along the way. First, the appetite to be part of this is bigger than a single round of sessions. And second, "normal" hours simply don't work for everyone. We heard from residents for whom, between work and young children, the only free moment in the day comes after bedtime. So we listened, and we built this next round around that.

Alongside more daytime and in-person sessions, we've added late-evening virtual sessions at 9:00 PM that you can join from your couch after the house goes quiet. No babysitter, no commute — just you, your ideas, and a screen.

Go deeper: join a focus group

The listening sessions are broad, open conversations. Focus groups go deeper. Each one brings together people who share a particular connection to the library — a love of books, a craft, a profession, a stage of life — to talk in detail about how specific spaces should look, feel, and work in the renovated buildings.

Browse the groups below and express interest in any that speak to you. You can join more than one, and you don't need to be an expert — just someone who cares. After you sign up, we'll follow up with details about when and how each group will meet.
